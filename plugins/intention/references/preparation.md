# Prepare at the execution frontier

Activation is permission; preparation establishes that the work still fits.
This policy applies to run, direct act, and every member of a run-wave.
Python observes eligibility; the conductor performs the semantic check.

## Authority

“Run this whole intention” and “activate this set” (or “activate what we
just intended”) grant continuing write/activation authority for that named
intention within its agreed goals, non-goals, and architectural
commitments. Record the user's instruction and those bounds on the root
bead (or the existing DAG in chat when there is no tracker). Do not invent
a campaign store. Bare `/run`, an old ACTIVE BUILD banner, and “activate
all” do not grant authority to broaden scope. Existing activation remains
valid only within the scope approved. `--autonomous` / `--go` skip the
steer pause; they are not themselves a grant. ASK/EYES boxes stay
unchecked — review afterwards is `map` / `demo` / `status`.

Only `change` applies delegated activation, after preparation. It may set
ACTIVE BUILD for a dependency-ready node covered by the recorded grant.
Do not pre-activate blocked descendants. Explicit node activation remains
supported, but never waives preparation. Missing authority leaves PENDING.
PARKED, sensitive permission, unresolved ambiguity, explicit human gates,
and ASK/EYES obligations cannot be bypassed by campaign authority.

## Preparation

Before a node's first dispatch, and again after relevant inputs change:

1. Read its goal, assumptions, acceptance, dependencies, upstream signed
   results/evidence and usable artifacts, current code/specs, and relevant
   learnings. Verify all inbound dependencies are satisfied; a closed node
   alone is insufficient if its required artifact is unusable. Unresolved
   input means defer this branch, not guess.
2. Follow `change` for change/architecture/instrument rigor; `brief` for
   brief rigor; a direct-fix check for vibe/restore-only work. Reconcile the
   proposed contract with those inputs. No mandatory debrief wave.
3. Record a short preparation note in the existing bead or proposal:
   upstream result/commit references, relevant spec/code anchors, outcome
   (`still valid`, `revised`, or `replan`), and authority source/bounds.
   A still-valid proposal needs no rewritten deltas or tasks. Reuse a note
   only after checking its referenced inputs and contract are unchanged;
   unrelated HEAD commits do not invalidate it. Resumed sessions check too.
4. Routine refinements within authority continue. Scope expansion, changed
   architectural commitments, or an invalid goal require a handoff to
   intend/steer and the human decision; do not implement the stale node.
   Propose splits/removals and preserve dependency edges until resolved.
   Keep unrelated eligible nodes moving.
5. A revised architecture/instrument contract needs fresh advise; an old
   accept does not cover it. Add the exact owed task `- [ ] Fresh advise after preparation`
   until the new reader accepts; status treats it as needs_advise even
   when a historical accept exists, preventing stale dispatch/fold.
   Do not clear unrelated ASK/EYES boxes. Record the review's contract
   reference. Unchanged contracts retain their valid review.
6. Re-observe after changing a proposal, dependencies, activation, or review
   state. Dispatch only if preparation, authority, readiness, and required
   review all hold. An unchanged preparation may proceed in this wave;
   do not loop through change forever just because the card still says act.

Before a parallel wave, prepare every candidate, then regenerate packets
and recompute disjointness. Preparation of one candidate never covers its
siblings. Workers receive the reconciled contract and inherited facts.
