## One real tradeoff

**Mint vs select.** Both are allowed (`specs/dossier/spec.md:41-42`).
Mint is a new intention. Select binds an existing one. The fence is
the cite: emerge without a dossier citation is a defect
(`specs/dossier/spec.md:66-70`). Cost: a hostile select could stamp
provenance onto unrelated work. Benefit: you do not have to fork an
intention that already exists. Hold the cite. Do not let select mean
a back-dated stamp.
