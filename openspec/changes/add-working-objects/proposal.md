# add-working-objects

> **ACTIVE BUILD**

Depends on: `extract-working-method`.

## Why

The seven objects and the brief shape are how the method talks. They
live in the founding doc §§ Objects. Packet/result are already
`agent-surface`. This slice is the *work* objects: intention,
capability, change, work node, evidence, learning — and that a brief
dies after landing.

## What

ADD those objects onto `working-method`. Do not fork `agent-surface`.

## Impact

- Capabilities: MODIFIED `working-method`
- ADRs: none

## User journey & surfaces

No new UI because the surfaces are briefs, beads, and living specs.
Working: a unit of work is a brief or a change, not a story template.
Empty: they write a 200-line story. Failed: a brief is kept as
durable truth. Off: leave PENDING.

- Packet/result field tables (`agent-surface`)
- Two-layer tree (`living-specs`)
