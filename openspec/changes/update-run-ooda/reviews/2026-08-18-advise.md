observe-only and testable. A free-form goal (`/run we need
extract-from`) then goes to `intend`. The cost is a false
`next: change` on a kebab that is not a landing (`/run
please-fix-ready`). The other side — “if it has spaces it is a
goal” — fails closed on a pasted change-id and fails open on a
one-word wish. Name the detector here. Do not let act pick it.
