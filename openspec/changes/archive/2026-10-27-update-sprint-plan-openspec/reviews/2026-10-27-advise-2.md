## Blind take

1. Pin explicit user selection of the factory as the invocation boundary.
2. Pin `intend` as the destination for a generic planning request.
3. Refuse treating the desire for a thorough plan as implicit factory selection.
4. Pin one change directory as the authoritative home of plan narrative.
5. Pin `tasks.md` checkboxes as phase progress, including continuation state.
6. Refuse new `docs/sprints/`, `.omc/sprint-plan/`, or `phase-state.json` plan stores.
7. Require initialization to resolve a change identity without old sprint directories or symlinks.
8. Require explicit treatment of durable architecture decisions and child work references without duplicating the plan.
9. Keep story-bead policy in its sibling change and preserve the parked living contract until implementation and fold.
10. Tradeoff: human-readable change documents replace a dedicated state machine, so phase resumption must remain unambiguous.

## Comparison and steelman

`design.md`, or `tasks.md`. This is a review of the prepared contract,
not a claim that the skill has been implemented.

Both requested corrections are satisfied:

- **Invocation:** the modified requirement limits intake to `/sprint-plan`
  or a planning session that names the factory. It expressly routes generic
  planning to `intend` and prohibits starting Phase 0. Both invocation
  scenarios agree with `design.md`'s Invocation section, and
  `disable-model-invocation: true` remains required.
- **Output boundary:** both delta requirements distinguish plan narrative
  and phase progress from an ADR in `ARCHITECTURE.md` and a child bead or
  child change id. This agrees with phases 2A and 2B in the design. The
  shape-change condition bounds the ADR; child artifacts are handoffs.
  Story-bead work remains assigned to `update-sprint-plan-beads`.

The strongest case against my initial boundary concern is that a single
plan store need not prohibit every external output. An architectural
decision or child-work identity can be external without becoming a second
session plan. The amendment states that distinction explicitly while
retaining the exclusive location of narrative and progress, so a stricter
ban would obstruct the phase map without improving plan ownership.

The design also answers initialization and state concerns: mint one
verb-led change id, edit the same directory through later phases, and use
checked phase boxes as the cursor. The removal task includes old directory
writes and `phase-state.json`. These are implementation obligations still
open; they do not contradict the prepared contract. The cited skill's
parked banner and old paths remain expected at this review stage.
