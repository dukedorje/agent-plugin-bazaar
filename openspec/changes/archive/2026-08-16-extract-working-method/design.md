# extract-working-method — why a new capability

`verbs` is what the dispatchers produce. `living-specs` is the
two-layer tree. `agent-surface` is packet-in / result-out.
`default-loop` is what a stranger is offered first.

None of those is the user-facing algebra: OODA, rigor as a dial,
the seven objects, split rules. Stuffing that into `verbs` would
make the dispatcher spec a novel again.

`working-method` is that algebra. Children add requirements. The
founding doc becomes a pointer after `update-founding-doc-pointer`.
