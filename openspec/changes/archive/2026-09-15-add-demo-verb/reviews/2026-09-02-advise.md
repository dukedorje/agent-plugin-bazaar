## Steelman

The author could say: the journey source is obvious (the example
literally says "points at `/run-wave`", the Next command on the
run-wave EYES box), and the archive case is `update-run-demo`'s
problem because that is where demo meets the campaign. Half right.
The source *is* obvious to a reader who has the run-wave box open;
it is not obvious to the Sonnet worker who gets this delta as a
packet — density is inverse of capability. And the archive case is
not campaign logic; it is the meaning of "landed" in a slice that
explicitly ships without a campaign hook, so `/demo` will mostly be
typed *after* a roll has folded. The contrast in the packet
("Duke can demo without folding") only holds if demo survives
someone else having folded.
